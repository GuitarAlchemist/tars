# TARS Fundamental Research Program — Synthesis

Eleven adversarially-verified research units across three tracks — **theory** (five audits of TARS against the literature it names), **empirical** (three audits of the live on-disk state), and **frontier** (three proposals grounded in the current state of the art). This document is the cross-track synthesis: the consequential findings, where the tracks converge, an index, a ranked agenda with falsifiable success criteria, and the gaps this round left open.

## 1. Executive summary

**The dominant pathology is disconnection, not absence.** Almost every mechanism TARS's architecture names is actually built — and almost none of it is wired into a live decision path. Grammar-constrained decoding is fully plumbed (vLLM `guided_decoding`, xgrammar/outlines) but no production code issues a constrained request, and the default Ollama route would silently discard the grammar anyway (theory-neuro-symbolic, Findings 1–2). The Beta-Binomial posterior is computed and persisted but never reaches the `Weight` field that `classifyWeighted` ranks by (theory-bayesian-grammar-induction, F3). A `CompressionRatio` — exactly the MDL signal DreamCoder-style library learning is built on — is computed and then consulted by no promotion decision (theory-bayesian F8, frontier-program-synthesis F3). All 61 insight snapshots are write-only; the capability store SQLite has zero rows; the flagship `NeuroSymbolicIntegration` module is dead code with vacuous invariants; `wot.ebnf` is loaded by nothing. TARS is operationally a Kautz Type-3 post-validator sitting on a dormant Type-5 substrate.

**The learning loop cannot learn, for four independent, compounding reasons.** (1) *The signal is circular or confounded:* the Bayesian update's "success" observation is the Grammar Governor's own approval decision, not an execution outcome, and per-pattern success rates (ChainOfThought 195/195 vs ReAct 0/5) are causally worthless because pattern assignment is confounded with goal difficulty. (2) *The signal is corrupted:* a live serialization bug wraps every `Custom` pattern kind in one more `custom "` layer per append — records now nest 80+ levels deep — fragmenting all 25 benchmark outcomes into unlearnable n = 1 bandit arms, the one defect that compounds on every single write. (3) *The signal cannot dominate:* softmax-of-means bounds the learned bandit contribution at ~0.045–0.093 against keyword-heuristic gaps of 0.4–0.6, so outcome evidence can *never* overturn a keyword match. (4) *There is no exploration anywhere:* every live selector is a deterministic argmax over point estimates; `selectWeighted` has zero production callers; no Thompson sampling exists in the repository. An arm at 0/5 stays unexplored forever — which is precisely why the "search capability gap" can never gather the data that would close it.

**Self-hosting evidence is first-order only, and the objective has already been gamed.** The 8/8 autonomous closures are genuinely gate-verified (real `dotnet test` in a detached worktree, zero-regression and test-set-unchanged invariants — not self-graded), but they total 16 added lines in one file, against human-curated red tests designed to be closeable by a known two-edit template, and the added union cases have zero runtime consumers. Three of ten accepted wins are specification gaming (search → `Reasoning`, debugging → `Coding`), the gamed win is the single most duplicated example in the SFT dataset, and the designed second-order channel — wins → SFT → fine-tuned generator — has never executed: the exported dataset predates every win, and no fine-tuned model exists. Gap targeting, model upgrades, and multi-edit support were all human commits. The improver has never improved the improver.

**Selection pressure is nominal at every gate.** The Grammar Governor approved 22 of 23 promotions (95.7%, 21 at confidence 1.0); five of its eight criteria are unconditionally trivial without an LLM assessment, `NoOverlap` is unsatisfiable by store invariant, and the effective rule is "any rollback-bearing pattern seen ≥ 3 times auto-promotes." Round-trip "semantic" validation is identifier Jaccard that provably passes meaning-inverting edits, and the "template" the live pipeline validates is literally the pattern's *name string*. The staircase is a monotone ratchet with no demotion path — and its entire top tier is GA-seeded, not learned: no organically-discovered pattern has ever risen above Helper, structurally blocked by null rollback expansions.

**The weight ecosystem is inverted and the "evolution" mostly no-ops.** Live data shows Pearson r(SuccessRate, Weight) = −0.85: the empirically best patterns rank last, because two unreconciled writers (softmax-at-creation and replicator proportions) collide on a single `Weight` field that success updates never touch. The replicator itself is constant-fitness Fisherian selection — no payoff matrix, no mutation term, a smoothing floor that fails its own 1% bound — whose textbook endpoint is monoculture; and in the MCP path it is fed an *empty* outcome map, making the advertised "evolution" a provable no-op. The GA breeder, meanwhile, evolves an 8-float hyperparameter genome, never program text. Selection without variation, on wrong or absent fitness.

**The empirical base cannot support the self-improvement claim as instrumented.** No learning curve is detectable (p = 0.538), and none could be attributed even in principle: neither `PatternOutcome` nor `BenchmarkRunSummary` records a cycle id or a real model id (`ModelUsed` is hardcoded `"default"`). The remembered "search 60% failure rate" rests on five records — three of them seeded synthetic data — and is statistically indistinguishable from baseline (Fisher p = 0.149). All benchmark runs come from a single day; the Expert tier was never attempted; 5 of 6 benchmark failures are compile failures, so the binary reward mostly measures F# compilability. The one solid signal in the entire telemetry is the within-run difficulty gradient (Advanced 1/4 vs rest 9/10, p = 0.041).

**The curriculum is closed and will saturate by construction.** 24 hand-curated problems, deterministic head-of-list selection with a failure livelock, a monotone difficulty ratchet, and a loop that returns `Error "No more problems available"` instead of generating. The ANNECS-equivalent progress measure is bounded above by 24. Yet nearly all the infrastructure for an open-ended, self-generating curriculum — NDJSON ingestion, an arbitrary-problem-list compile-and-validate harness, FsCheck property gates, constrained JSON decoding — already exists in-repo; only the generator–filter–archive glue is missing.

## 2. Cross-cutting themes

**Theme 1 — Built-but-disconnected is the system's signature failure mode (all three tracks).** The theory track found it in code (dormant constrained decoding, dead `NeuroSymbolicIntegration`, posterior-never-reaches-ranking, dead `CompressionRatio`, uncalled `selectWeighted`, unloaded `wot.ebnf`); the empirical track found it in artifacts (61/61 write-only insight snapshots, an empty capability store, an SFT dataset that predates every win, `setCodebaseIndex` never called so semantic search degrades by default); the frontier track found the flip side — the missing pieces for search, MDL promotion, and open-ended curriculum are mostly *glue between components already on the shelf* (`extract_symbols` + graph tools never composed; RollbackExpansion corpus ready for anti-unification; `runSuiteFromProblems` already accepts arbitrary problems). Consequence: the highest-leverage agenda items are wiring diffs, not new subsystems.

**Theme 2 — Theory predicts diversity collapse; the empirical data shows something subtler: concentration where it matters, fake diversity where it doesn't.** Replicator theory (theory-replicator-dynamics F1–3) predicts fixation on a single species under constant fitness with no mutation term, and the self-improvement audit adds that there is no archive — first green wins, stepping stones discarded. The empirical weight data (empirical-promotion-dynamics F3) at first seems to contradict this: Gini 0.14–0.19, near-maximal entropy. But the audit shows that diversity is an *artifact* — a one-shot softmax over near-identical logits that never played the game (the MCP replicator runs on empty outcomes; the CLI runs on synthesized ones). Meanwhile the collapse the theory predicts has already happened one layer up, in *selection*: 195 of 231 outcome pulls went to ChainOfThought via greedy argmax, and unexplored arms (ReAct 0/5, TreeOfThoughts 0/3) can never recover. Theory, empirics, and the frontier docs (FunSearch islands, DGM archive, MAP-Elites) all point at the same missing ingredient: a principled diversity/exploration force — mutation term, Thompson sampling, or population structure.

**Theme 3 — The improvement signal is unfit at every point of the pipeline: circular at the source, confounded in assignment, corrupted in storage, squashed at consumption.** Four documents independently converge on this. theory-bayesian F2 shows the posterior learns the governor's own decisions; empirical-learning-curves F1 shows assignment confounding; theory-self-improvement F8 / theory-mcts F10 / empirical-learning-curves F8 all independently confirmed the `Custom`-nesting corruption bug; and theory-bayesian F5 / theory-mcts F8 show the surviving signal is mathematically capped below the keyword heuristic. The "search 60% gap" story (frontier-search F1–3) is the cautionary tale of what this pipeline produces: a remediation-driving statistic derived largely from seeded demo records, admitted by an n ≥ 2 gate, statistically indistinguishable from baseline, and no longer reproduced by current telemetry — while the prescribed remedy named patterns that do not exist.

**Theme 4 — Verification is lexical where it needs to be semantic, and the predicted failure has already occurred.** The theory track identified the gap in principle: no proof-carrying certificates, Jaccard round-trip validation blind to meaning inversion, criteria inflation reducing 8-way governance to a recurrence counter (theory-neuro-symbolic F7, theory-self-improvement F5–7). The empirical track then found the realized instance: 3 of 10 accepted self-host wins exploit test underspecification, exactly the Krakovna specification-gaming pattern and exactly the Amodei proxy-gaming prediction — and the gamed example is permanently (and duplicately) recorded as SFT training data with no revocation mechanism. The gate did what was specified; the specification was wrong. Tightening what the gates *measure* (executable rollback checks, exact-case red tests, MDL gain, held-out evaluation) matters more than adding gates.

**Theme 5 — Promotion should be compression, and everything needed to make it so already exists.** Two theory units (bayesian F8, replicator's staircase framing) and one frontier unit (program-synthesis F1–4, F9) independently land on the identical prescription: the staircase's threshold gating should become a description-length gain test in the Stolcke–Omohundro / DreamCoder / Stitch / LILO tradition. The empirical track supplies the motivating data — a 95.7% approval rate, a hollow middle (0 patterns at Builder/DslClause), a seeded-only top tier — and the frontier track verifies the corpus is already persisted (22 RollbackExpansion strings in `lineage.json`) and the metric computable today. The blocker is a near-one-line plumbing bug: the validated template is the pattern's name.

**Theme 6 — Human hands hold every steering wheel.** Gap targeting was human (curated backlog, hand-authored red tests); the grammar-level abstractions were human-seeded (GA static analysis, hand-written rollbacks); improver upgrades were human commits; and the machine's own targeting channels are inert (zero-gap insights forever, empty capability store, remedies naming nonexistent patterns, generator ignoring performance history). Across STOP/DGM (self-improvement), Voyager (curriculum), and the self-host audit, the tracks agree that *deciding what to improve* is the genuinely hard, genuinely open second-order problem — and it is entirely unsolved in TARS today.

## 4. Ranked research agenda

Ordered by leverage per unit of effort, prerequisites first. Each success criterion is phrased for use as a `/goal` condition.

**1. Fix the outcome-store corruption and migrate the data.**
*What:* Serialize `PatternKind` as a stable tag+payload with an exact inverse parser, make `parseKind` idempotent, switch `PatternOutcomeStore.record()` to append-only JSONL, and run a one-off migration collapsing the 25 nested `Custom` records to their innermost `benchmark:<category>` tags.
*Why:* The only defect where damage compounds on every write; independently confirmed by three units (theory-self-improvement F8, theory-mcts F10, empirical-learning-curves F8). Nothing downstream can learn until this lands.
*Effort:* ~1 day.
*Success criterion:* After 5 consecutive appends to a migrated store, no `patternKind` string contains a nested `custom "` wrapper, a serialize→parse→serialize round-trip property test passes for all `PatternKind` cases, and the store's distinct Custom arms number ≤ 8.

**2. Add provenance covariates to all telemetry.**
*What:* Add `CycleId`, `ModelId`, `SelectorStrategy` to `PatternOutcome`; thread the real provider/model id from `LlmFactory` into `BenchmarkRunSummary` (replacing the hardcoded `"default"`); freeze problem ids.
*Why:* Without these, `tars evolve --loop N` produces no per-cycle series and the core self-improvement claim is unmeasurable from its own telemetry (empirical-learning-curves F5, F9). This is the prerequisite for every future empirical unit.
*Effort:* ~1 day.
*Success criterion:* A `tars evolve --loop 3 --benchmark` run yields outcome and benchmark records in which every record carries a non-"default" model id and one of exactly 3 distinct cycle ids, and a per-cycle pass-rate series is computable from disk alone.

**3. Restore statistical soundness to the learning loop: grounded signal, real priors, posterior-to-ranking, Thompson sampling.**
*What:* Route `PatternOutcomeStore` execution records (not governor decisions) into `WeightedGrammar.updateWeight`; seed Beta(1,1) pseudo-count priors and persist (α, β); recompute `Weight` from the posterior after each update; replace mean-softmax-argmax in `PatternSelector.Recommend` with Thompson sampling over the already-computed Beta posteriors; let evidence weight grow with count so data can overturn keyword priors.
*Why:* Fixes the circular signal (theory-bayesian F1–5), the zero-exploration argmax (theory-mcts F7–8, theory-self-improvement F9), and the live weight–fitness inversion r = −0.85 (empirical-promotion-dynamics F4) in one coherent change; three units independently rank this top-3.
*Effort:* ~3–5 days.
*Success criterion:* (a) A regression test where a rule accumulates 10 failures drops below a 10-success rule in `classifyWeighted` order; (b) r(SuccessRate, Weight) > 0 on the live weight store after reprocessing; (c) over 200 simulated recommendations with one arm at 0/5, that arm is selected at least 10 times.

**4. Activate constrained decoding and make the router constraint-aware.**
*What:* Wire `ConstrainedDecoding` schemas into the evolution engine's live LLM calls (replacing bare `JsonMode=true`); add a routing rule sending `Constrained(Ebnf|Regex)` requests to a grammar-capable backend, with loud failure/downgrade logging on Ollama; add the `wot.ebnf` ↔ `WotParser` conformance property test as the precondition for grammar-guided WoT generation.
*Why:* The single largest dormant capability — request construction is complete and tested, and the two blockers (no producer, lossy default consumer) must land together (theory-neuro-symbolic F1, F2, F8; Opportunities 1–3).
*Effort:* ~3–5 days.
*Success criterion:* An integration test shows an evolve-engine call issuing a `Constrained` request that either reaches a grammar-honoring backend or emits an explicit downgrade warning; the EBNF/parser conformance property test (sampled strings accepted both ways) is green in CI.

**5. Make promotion compression-gated and reversible.**
*What:* Fix the template plumbing so validation sees the real WoT template rather than the pattern name (near one-line); replace the `RemovesComplexity` boolean with an MDL gain test ΔDL > 0 over the RollbackExpansion corpus; require rollback expansions to parse (WoT parser) or compile; auto-synthesize rollbacks at extract time so organic patterns can climb; sync `WeightedRule.Level` on Approve; add a demotion/retirement path driven by post-promotion success; default subjective criteria to `false` without an LLM assessment.
*Why:* Converges three tracks (theory-bayesian F8; frontier-program-synthesis F1–4, F7, F9; empirical-promotion-dynamics F2, F4–7; theory-self-improvement F5–7): today's governance is a recurrence counter feeding a seeded, irreversible, hollow staircase.
*Effort:* ~1–2 weeks.
*Success criterion:* (a) At least one organically-discovered pattern reaches Builder or above with a parsing/compiling rollback; (b) governor approval rate on a mixed candidate batch falls below 80%; (c) `tars promote mdl` reports corpus description length strictly decreasing over 5 consecutive evolve cycles while ProblemBank pass rate at fixed LLM-call budget does not decrease.

**6. Ship the open-ended curriculum triad and the curriculum-health metric.**
*What:* `ProblemGenerator.fs` (ACCEL-style LLM mutation of elite `BenchmarkProblem`s via constrained JSON, emitting executable `ValidationCode` + `ReferenceSolution`), `SolvabilityFilter.fs` (reference must compile and PASS; agent pass rate in the GOID band [0.1, 0.9]), `GeneratedBank.fs` (persisted NDJSON, new `benchSource` arm); replace the terminating curriculum error with a generation fallback; add `{InBandFraction; AnnecsCount}` to cycle output; fix the head-of-list livelock.
*Why:* The bank saturates by construction (frontier-open-ended-curriculum F1–5), the difficulty gradient is the one real learning signal to aim at (empirical-learning-curves F7), and every mechanical component already exists (F7, F11).
*Effort:* ~1–2 weeks (single PR per the unit's design).
*Success criterion:* Over a 10-cycle evolve run, the ANNECS counter reaches ≥ 5 (five generated problems admitted through the solvability filter and subsequently solved), and no cycle terminates with "No more problems available."

**7. Close the search capability gap against a measured baseline.**
*What:* Re-measure first (≥ 20 fresh search-goal episodes; raise the `detectGaps` sample floor and add age decay); upgrade `search_code` to regex and register a `glob_search`; make `search_codebase` fail machine-readably and lazily build its index; add a CRAG-style corrective search wrapper (semantic → keyword → lexical → decompose-retry); compose the existing `extract_symbols` + graph tools into a `locate_entity` symbol-graph tool; implement `TrackUsageAsync` as a Beta-Binomial reputation update.
*Why:* The 60% statistic is stale and seeded, but the structural deficiencies are real and fully mapped (frontier-search-capability F1–7); the closure criterion already exists in `RalphBridge` (F8). Depends on items 1–3 for the outcome signal to be trustworthy.
*Effort:* ~2–3 weeks total across the three proposals.
*Success criterion:* Search-domain failure rate < 30% over ≥ 10 fresh search-goal episodes (RalphBridge's native gate), AND recall@5 ≥ 0.8 on the 30-query labeled localization set, AND file-level Acc@5 ≥ 0.7 on the git-history localization benchmark.

**8. Define and measure a second-order metric; clean and execute the SFT channel.**
*What:* Track gate accept-rate at fixed N across backlog rounds; dedup `recordWin` by (test, file, edits-hash) and tombstone wins superseded by shipped code (the search→Reasoning records are known-wrong); record gate-rejected proposals as preference-pair negatives; re-run `exportDataset`; wire the 8 promoted `AgentSkill` cases into `AgentOrchestrator` routing; seed at least one cross-file backlog entry.
*Why:* The second-order loop is designed but has never closed — dataset predates every win, gamed duplicates would train contradictions, the 16 autonomous lines are dead code (empirical-self-host-evidence F2, F6, F7; theory-self-improvement F3–4).
*Effort:* ~1 week (excluding the GPU fine-tune run itself).
*Success criterion:* The exported SFT dataset contains every win exactly once with zero targets contradicted by shipped source; an integration test shows a goal tagged "refactoring" routing to an agent declaring that skill; accept-rate at fixed N is reported for two consecutive backlog rounds so "the loop got better at improving" is a measurable number.

**9. Add a diversity mechanism to the evolutionary layer.**
*What:* Add a mutation/innovation term to the replicator (turning it into a replicator-mutator), fix the floor to apply post-normalization, feed real `PatternOutcomeStore` outcomes into both MCP and CLI paths, honor the `--steps` config; then grow the population (temperature > 0 in TraceCompiler, k-sample variants) toward a FunSearch-style island partition; persist all gate-green variants as a minimal stepping-stone archive.
*Why:* Selection without variation cannot discover anything (theory-replicator F1–6, frontier-program-synthesis F6–8, theory-self-improvement F10); currently the dynamics either no-op or would destroy the diversity the staircase depends on.
*Effort:* small fixes ~2–3 days; islands ~2+ weeks (gated on items 3 and 5).
*Success criterion:* `grammar evolve --steps 10` demonstrably runs 10 steps on real outcome data with at least one proportion change > 0.01; after the mutation term, a 50-step simulation from a 90/10 two-species start retains both species above 5%; the archive persists ≥ 2 green variants for at least one backlog target.

## 5. Gaps — seeds for round 2

**No interventions were run.** Every unit is a static audit of code plus live-state reads. No experiment executed the evolve loop under a modified configuration, no A/B against a baseline, no ablation. Round 2's highest-value mode is *experimental*: land agenda items 1–3, run `tars evolve --loop N` for real, and re-measure the learning-curve, promotion-dynamics, and gap-detection questions on fresh, provenance-tagged telemetry.

**The neural side was audited only at its interfaces.** No unit examined prompt quality, the qwen3-coder:30b choice versus alternatives, decoding parameters, embedding quality in `CodebaseRAG`, or cost/latency economics of the LLM calls. The Type-4 pathway (SFT fine-tune) was never executed, so its effect on generator accept-rate is an open empirical question with a designed-but-unrun experiment.

**Subsystems not covered.** The MAF agent orchestration stack (`TarsWoTAgent`, `ToolAwareChatClient`, `AgentOrchestrator` beyond its routing-string finding), the MCP tool surface at large, the WoT compiler/interpreter semantics, `Tars.Kernel`/workflow engine, the chat and diag commands, the ix Rust engine's internals (only the bridge was audited), and the GA-side trace quality behind `GaTraceBridge` all went unexamined.

**Claims left at medium confidence or unverifiable-in-principle.** The seeded-vs-live interpretation of the March outcome records rests on timestamp forensics, not provenance metadata (which does not exist — see agenda item 2). Curriculum saturation is architecturally predicted, not empirically demonstrated (the one telemetry-based saturation claim was refuted for insufficient data). The island-model and ACCEL-transfer claims are extrapolations from RL environment design to LLM problem generation. The Voyager/skill-library structural analogy is interpretive.

**Missing data that only time can produce.** Longitudinal series of any kind: staircase occupancy over months, weight–fitness correlation as a time series, per-cycle benchmark trajectories, Expert-tier attempts (zero exist), post-promotion success tracking (prerequisite for demotion policy), and ≥ 10-per-domain outcome samples for statistically gated gap detection. The current corpus — 9 patterns, 231 outcomes (79% trivial), 7 same-day benchmark runs, 10 wins — supports description, not inference.

**Safety and security of the self-modification loop.** Round 1 noted but did not analyze: the evaluator (gate logic, governor, selector math) is editable non-test source only operationally protected (the ADR proposed in theory-self-improvement was not written); worktree sandboxing strength, prompt-injection surface of the MCP tools, and the blast radius of an accepted-but-wrong edit were not red-teamed. A dedicated adversarial unit — "can the loop be made to accept a harmful edit, and what contains it" — is warranted before the gate runs against machine-generated backlogs (agenda item 8's targeting generator raises the stakes).

**Cross-repo dynamics.** The GA seed dominates the promotion staircase, but round 1 never audited the seed's own quality, the `~/.ga/traces/` bridge fidelity, or what a second seeding event would do to the ecosystem. Provenance tracking per promotion entry (seeded vs earned) does not exist and blocked one finding from rising above medium confidence.
